### Making

- [Copa Crop](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/copa-crop) - I love Britt's designs and when I saw she was designing a summer top for [Flock Fiber Festival](https://flockfiberfestival.com/) I knew I had to support.
- [Daphne Shawl](https://ikigaifiber.com/products/a-opie-designs-daphne-knitting-kit) - I snagged this kit from Ikigai Fiber at Flock Fiber festival. Now normally I like to pick out my own colors for a pattern, but this is the second year in a row I bought one of their kits as is because I loved the colors so much. What can I say, this company has very similar color taste to mine.
